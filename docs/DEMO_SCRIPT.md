# Portfolio demo recording guide

## Goal

Record a polished 60–75 second video that demonstrates the problem, the product loop, and the quality of the mobile experience. Avoid presenting mock data as live production data.

## Before recording

- Use an iPhone 17 Pro Simulator in portrait orientation.
- Restart the app so the welcome screen appears first.
- Hide unrelated windows, notifications, and personal information.
- Set the Simulator scale so the entire phone is visible.
- Record only the Simulator window or a tight portion of the screen.
- Move the cursor slowly and pause briefly after each tap.

Use macOS screen recording with `Shift + Command + 5`, select **Record Selected Portion**, frame the Simulator, and begin recording.

## 60-second walkthrough

### 0–6 seconds — premise

Show the welcome screen.

Voiceover or caption:

> King of the Court helps local basketball players find games, build squads, and compete for their court's ranking.

Tap **Create an account**.

### 7–16 seconds — player home

Pause on the player rating and nearby activity. Scroll just enough to show the next game and court activity.

> The home screen combines a player's competitive profile with games and active courts nearby.

### 17–29 seconds — find a game

Open **Play**, switch between **1v1** and **Team**, then accept or request a game.

> Players can filter individual or team matchups by distance, format, time, and skill range.

### 30–43 seconds — court identity

Open **Courts**, select Christie Pits, scroll through the ranking, and tap **Challenge the king**.

> Every court has live activity, a local leaderboard, and a crown that can only be taken through a verified 1v1 challenge.

### 44–54 seconds — build a squad

Open **Squad**, switch from 3v3 to 5v5, and invite a friend.

> Friends can form squads for formats from 2v2 through 5v5 and search for nearby opponents.

### 55–65 seconds — player identity

Open **Me** and scroll through attributes and friends.

> The prototype connects player identity, performance, relationships, and place in one mobile experience.

End on the player card or welcome-screen logo.

## Suggested captions

Keep captions short enough to read without pausing:

1. Find local basketball games
2. Match by distance and skill
3. See who is at the court
4. Build your squad
5. Challenge the King

## Screenshots to capture

Capture clean portrait screenshots of:

1. Welcome screen
2. Home/player rating
3. Game discovery
4. Live courts map
5. Court leaderboard and King
6. Squad builder
7. Player profile

Use the welcome screen as the first LinkedIn image and the court leaderboard as the second; together they communicate brand and differentiation quickly.
